THE BASICS

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan is a schedule for putting a chosen amount into a mutual fund regularly. The schedule does not guarantee a profit or remove investment risk.

THE BASICS

What is a mutual fund?

A mutual fund pools money from many people and invests it according to its stated plan. Its value can rise or fall with the investments it holds.

RISK

Why diversify?

Spreading money across different investments can reduce reliance on a single company or area. It cannot prevent all losses.

TIME

What does compounding mean?

When earnings stay invested, future growth can build on earlier growth. The effect depends on returns, time, fees and taxes—and returns are never certain.
