Below is a curated recommendation guide designed specifically for non-traders to know where to deploy capital safely and what high-risk traps to avoid.

Where to Invest Right Now

1. Flexi-Cap & Large-Cap Equity Mutual Funds High Priority

Why for beginners? Spreads your investment across India's top 100 financially solid companies (Reliance, HDFC, TCS, L&T). Managed by seasoned fund managers with low annual fees.

Horizon: 5+ Years Est. Return: 12-14% p.a.
2. Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) & Gold ETFs Recommended

Why for beginners? Gold acts as a natural shock absorber when stock markets fluctuate. SGBs also pay an extra 2.5% fixed annual interest backed by the Government of India.

Horizon: 3-8 Years Low Risk Cushion
3. Short-Duration Debt Funds & Fixed Deposits Safe Cash Flow

Why for beginners? Current RBI repo rates (6.50%) mean high fixed yields (7-8% p.a.). Perfect for storing emergency cash and short-term goals needing guaranteed safety.

Horizon: 1-3 Years Capital Preservation

What to Avoid Right Now

1. Speculative Penny Stocks & Social Media "Tips" High Risk Trap

Why avoid? Micro-cap penny stocks listed on Telegram or YouTube are frequently manipulated in "pump and dump" schemes. 95% of retail investors lose their capital here.

High Risk of 90%+ Capital Loss
2. Futures & Options (F&O) Day Trading Avoid Completely

Why avoid? Official SEBI study reveals that 9 out of 10 individual traders lose money in F&O derivatives. It is equivalent to high-stakes gambling for non-professionals.

SEBI Stat: 90% Net Loss
3. High-Cost Illiquid Schemes & Unregulated Crypto Caution

Why avoid? Traditional endowment plans offer meager 4-5% returns with 10-year lock-ins. Unregulated crypto platforms lack legal recourse if tokens crash or exchanges fail.

Illiquid & High Fees
